1. Purpose

This policy governs the selection, onboarding, and ongoing evaluation of third-party vendors engaged by the organization.

2. Vendor Risk Tiers

2.1 Tier 1 Vendors

Vendors with access to sensitive data or critical systems require annual security audits.

2.2 Tier 2 Vendors

Vendors without data access require only a standard due-diligence questionnaire at onboarding.

3. Approval Thresholds

The table below defines spend thresholds requiring additional approval.

Contract ValueApprover
Under $10,000Department Manager
$10,000 - $100,000VP + Procurement
Over $100,000CFO

4. Termination

Vendor contracts may be terminated for cause with 30 days written notice, per the terms of the master service agreement.
